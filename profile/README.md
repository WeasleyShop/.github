# Weasley.shop

### Find your next digital product.

Weasley.shop is a marketplace for apps, websites, and creative tools from independent makers. Explore projects, discover useful products, and connect with their creators.

- **Explore:** [Browse the marketplace](https://weasley.shop/)
- **For makers:** [Submit a project](https://weasley.shop/submit/type/)
- **Read:** [Articles](https://weasley.shop/articles/) and [News](https://weasley.shop/news/)
- **Contact:** [weasley@weasley.shop](mailto:weasley@weasley.shop)
